Project overview · draft

A marketplace for home-cooked food in Karachi

GharKaKhana (GKK) is a charitable home-food initiative run by Fazal-e-Insaaniyat Charity Works (FI). It connects home chefs who cook from their own kitchens with customers who want honest, home-cooked meals nearby, starting in Karachi. Chefs publish a kitchen profile and a menu; customers discover kitchens, browse dishes, and order.

What it is

A mobile app (Android and iOS via Expo) backed by Supabase, written in React Native and TypeScript, operated by Fazal-e-Insaaniyat Charity Works (FI), a family-run charity.

🍛

A community marketplace

Chefs publish what they cook; customers browse and order. Two interfaces, one shared account and backend.

📍

Location-aware

Customers see kitchens near them, filtered by radius and sorted by distance, rating, or name.

📱

Karachi first

Prices in PKR, +92 phone handling, and a launch focused on Karachi. Settlement is manual: cash on delivery, JazzCash, Easypaisa, or bank transfer.

Who it serves

👩‍🍳

Home chefs

  • Set up a kitchen with a name, bio, photos, address, and cuisines.
  • Publish a menu with prices, dietary tags, and spice levels.
  • Toggle "accepting orders" on and off at any time.
🛍️

Customers

  • Discover active kitchens near their location.
  • Filter and sort the list; browse each kitchen's menu.
  • Order by calling the kitchen from inside the app.

How it works

The app is organised around three shared pieces: accounts, a chef side, and a customer side.

1 · Account

Create an account as a customer or chef. The app gates every screen on your session and role.

2 · Chef side

A three-step onboarding wizard, then a dashboard for editing kitchen details and managing dishes.

3 · Customer side

A home screen of nearby kitchens, each with a menu page. Ordering today is by phone call; simple in-app ordering is planned.

Read the honest feature breakdown

What exists today is documented in FEATURES.md, labelled complete, partial, or stub. A few things that look like features are not yet working:

  • The search bar and cart button on the customer home are decorative and have no handlers.
  • The Orders tabs on both home screens are placeholders ("Orders tab content here").
  • Ordering works only by phone call today; in-app ordering and settlement are planned, not built.
  • Dish photos added on a phone stay unverified end to end from this repository; the upload and display paths have been aligned on a single stored shape.
